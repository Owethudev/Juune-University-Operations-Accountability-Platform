## Brand & Style

This design system delivers an institutional-grade, modern enterprise environment engineered for higher education administration, audit trails, and multi-departmental operations. The aesthetic is calm, authoritative, and strictly utilitarian—balancing high-density data requirements with visual clarity.

### Personality & Demeanor
- **Trustworthy & Resolute:** Conveys structural reliability and data fidelity. Avoids decorative novelties, prioritizing predictable, legible interactions.
- **Calm & Objective:** Operational phrasing is systematically non-accusatory, neutral, and clear. Escalations, reviews, and status alerts prioritize factual resolution over alarmist visual triggers.
- **High-Clarity Enterprise SaaS:** Information architecture supports rapid scanning across heavy tabular views, dense auditing logs, and tiered administrative privilege states.

### Visual Style
The design style executes a **Corporate Modern** philosophy grounded in low-contrast structural borders, functional white space, and subtle, tinted ambient elevation. It discards heavy dropshadows in favor of deliberate border outlines and crisp contrast steps, maintaining absolute legibility under high-density constraints.

## Colors

The palette establishes an institutional hierarchy rooted in maritime deep blues, cool slate neutrals, and balanced semantic indicators that guarantee AA/AAA compliance across all text and interaction states.

### Core Canvas & Surface System
- **Page Canvas (`#F7F9FB`):** A cool-tinted off-white reducing screen fatigue during prolonged administrative usage.
- **Elevated Surfaces (`#FFFFFF`):** Work surfaces, datagrid bodies, panels, and modals rely on pure white to contrast against the canvas.
- **Structural Dividing Borders (`#D8E0E7`):** Delimits tabular rows, card wrappers, and split-panes without visual noise.

### Brand & Interactive Hierarchy
- **Deep Navy Primary (`#18324B`):** Applied to primary navigation headers, high-level structural components, and primary solid call-to-actions.
- **Operations Blue (`#2F6690`):** Interactive links, focus indicators, filter controls, and active navigational tabs.
- **Accent Teal (`#3A7D7A`):** Used for tertiary highlights, secondary metrics, cross-department tags, and balanced data indicators.

### Content & Typography
- **Primary Text (`#17212B`):** Near-black slate ensuring extreme contrast for records and dense data tables.
- **Muted Text (`#5B6570`):** Secondary metadata, audit timestamps, helper hints, and table headers.

### Operational State & Feedback Semantics
- **Success (`#059669`):** Approved records, settled reconciliations, compliant statuses.
- **Warning (`#D97706`):** Approaching review deadlines, flagged variance items, non-critical warnings.
- **Danger / Destructive (`#9B2C2C`):** Non-compliance flags, authorization rejections, critical security access revocations.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy is built on **Inter** (backed by the system-native sans-serif fallbacks), selected for its engineered tabular figures, vertical alignment neutrality, and micro-legibility in complex administrative interfaces. For ledger references, serial tracking numbers, and audit IDs, a mono-spaced variant is assigned to guarantee column vertical stability.

### Hierarchy & Usage Notes
- **Headlines:** Keep visual weight to `600` (Semi-bold). Do not use extra-bold weights; academic leadership interfaces require measured authority without shouting.
- **Dense Data Tables:** Standard data rows resolve strictly at `body-md` (14px) and `body-sm` (13px), facilitating dense horizontal scans without sacrificing row readability.
- **Labels & Badges:** `label-xs` is utilized in uppercase or structured title case for least-privilege badges, security clearances, and operational workflow status indicators.
- **Numbers & Balances:** Enable `font-feature-settings: 'tnum' 1` globally for datagrids to prevent column jitters across dynamic numerical updates.

## Layout & Spacing

The platform uses a strict **4px/8px incremental grid system** configured for high-density enterprise displays, ensuring consistent spatial cadence between interactive toolbars, data filters, and layout containers.

### Grid & Layout Strategy
- **Master Desktop Shell:** Standard 12-column responsive layout anchored with a persistent 256px collapsible operations rail on the left. The main workspace operates with a minimum fluid width before triggering horizontal table virtualization.
- **Tablet / Split Screen (768px – 1024px):** 8-column layout with the side rail collapsed into a compact icon rail. Margin reduces to `1.5rem`.
- **Mobile Handheld (<768px):** 4-column layout; lateral margins scale down to `1rem`. Dense operational tables transition to expandable summary cards.

### Touch & Cursor Precision
Although spatial density inside desktop grids is compact, all standalone interactive items, mobile buttons, and data actions preserve an accessible **44px touch-target area** (either through physical height or transparent pointer padding) to support tablet operations during campus inspections and field operations.

## Elevation & Depth

To emphasize clinical precision, visual depth is managed primarily through **low-contrast borders (`#D8E0E7`)** and subtle white-on-slate tonal staging rather than heavy drop shadows.

### Surface Hierarchy
1. **Canvas (Base Level 0):** Background surface (`#F7F9FB`). Zero elevation.
2. **Structural Panels & Cards (Level 1):** Solid white surface (`#FFFFFF`) with a 1px solid `#D8E0E7` border. Shadows are absent or restricted to an ultra-soft ambient tint: `0 1px 2px 0 rgba(24, 50, 75, 0.04)`.
3. **Dropdowns, Menus & Tooltips (Level 2):** Layered above work areas with `0 4px 12px 0 rgba(24, 50, 75, 0.08)` and a 1px border (`#D8E0E7`).
4. **Modal Overlays & Critical Drawers (Level 3):** Modal surface (`#FFFFFF`) centered over a `rgba(23, 33, 43, 0.45)` backdrop. Container shadow: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(24, 50, 75, 0.16)`.

## Shapes

The geometric vocabulary is structured and formal:
- **Cards, Tables, and Structural Panels:** Fixed to `0.5rem` (`8px`) border radius, balancing crisp enterprise discipline with modern software standards.
- **Modals and Alert Dialogs:** Elevated to `0.75rem` (`12px`) border radius, imparting a clear contextual distinction between underlying pages and active modal interventions.
- **Inputs and Form Controls:** Strictly `0.25rem` (`4px`) or `0.375rem` (`6px`) radius to maximize tabular alignment and form density.
- **Pill Badges:** Fully circular (`9999px`) roundedness utilized exclusively for status chips and least-privilege administrative labels.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Deep navy (`#18324B`) background with white text (`#FFFFFF`). Min-height of 36px on desktop (44px target via wrapper), 44px on mobile. Active hover shifts to `#2F6690`.
- **Secondary / Outline:** White surface, 1px border (`#D8E0E7`), text `#17212B`. Hover triggers background `#F7F9FB`.
- **Destructive:** Border and text `#9B2C2C` with light red tint background (`#FEF2F2`) on hover; solid `#9B2C2C` reserved only for irreversible actions.
- **Focus Rings:** Distinct 2px outer outline in `#2F6690` with 2px white offset.

### Badges & Least-Privilege Indicators
- Compact, pill-shaped tags (`label-xs`) denoting role restrictions (e.g., `DEPT-ADMIN`, `AUDITOR-READONLY`, `COMPLIANCE-OFFICER`).
- Backgrounds use a 10% opacity tint of the respective semantic color, accompanied by high-contrast foreground text:
  - Read-Only / Neutral: `#F1F5F9` background, `#5B6570` text.
  - Active Clearance / Compliance: `#ECFDF5` background, `#059669` text.
  - Needs Review: `#FFFBEB` background, `#D97706` text.
  - Restricted / Revoked: `#FEF2F2` background, `#9B2C2C` text.

### Inputs & Selects
- Height of 36px (desktop) / 44px (touch). 1px border `#D8E0E7` on white fill.
- Placeholder text in `#5B6570` at 60% opacity.
- Focus: 1px border `#2F6690` with a 3px soft blue halo (`rgba(47, 102, 144, 0.15)`).
- Error state: 1px border `#9B2C2C` accompanied by an inline alert string directly underneath.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- 16px square (checkbox) or 16px circle (radio) with a 1px border `#D8E0E7` on `#FFFFFF`.
- Checked state: Fill `#18324B` with sharp white glyph.
- Interactive hit area padded out to 44px to satisfy touch criteria.

### Data Tables & Lists
- Table headers: `#F7F9FB` background, upper-border `#D8E0E7`, bottom-border `#D8E0E7`. Text set to `label-xs`, uppercase, `#5B6570`.
- Table rows: `#FFFFFF` background, 1px bottom border `#D8E0E7`. Row hover: `#F7F9FB`. Row height defaults to 44px for balanced density.

### Cards & Modals
- **Cards:** 8px border radius, 1px border `#D8E0E7`, background `#FFFFFF`. Padding rhythm strictly adheres to `1.25rem` (`20px`) or `1.5rem` (`24px`).
- **Modals:** 12px border radius, strict header-body-footer layout separated by 1px dividers (`#D8E0E7`). Header title strictly configured in `headline-sm` with non-accusatory operational copy (e.g., "Review Department Clearance Ledger" instead of "Authorization Warning").
